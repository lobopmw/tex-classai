\section*{Ethics Approval}

This study presents a proof-of-concept platform for observational learning analytics evaluated in a controlled laboratory environment. The proposed system is intended exclusively to support educational observation and does not perform psychological, medical, or clinical diagnosis.

\section*{Consent to Participate}

All demonstrations presented in this study involved volunteer members of the research laboratory who participated in the controlled proof-of-concept evaluation and authorized the use of their data for research purposes.
